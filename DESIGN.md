# Visual Aesthetic & Design System
**Brand:** Barhamthuri Aqua Solutions
**Theme:** High-end corporate professionalism fused with an immersive, water-inspired aesthetic (referencing the Brahmaputra river and Assam's natural environment).

## Color Palette
* **Primary Colors:** Deep Ocean Blue (#003B5C) and Crisp White (#FFFFFF) to convey purity.
* **Secondary/Accent Colors:** Cyan/Teal (#008B8B) for interactive elements, buttons, and highlights.
* **Contrasts:** Dark Charcoal (#333333) for footers and deep contrast typography.

## Typography & UI Elements
* **Headers:** Modern, clean sans-serif. Use oversized typography for hero sections. Include subtle clipping mask effects (text filled with water ripple imagery) for the main H1.
* **Body:** Minimalist, highly readable sans-serif for product specifications.
* **Geometry:** Avoid standard boxy frames. Use modern, geometric image masks (diagonal cuts, soft rounded water-droplet edges) for product photography.
* **Components:** Utilize a card-based grid system for e-commerce products. Cards should feature a white background, soft drop shadow, and gentle elevation hover effects.

## Animations
* Smooth, fluid transitions between pages.
* Parallax scrolling for background water imagery to create depth against foreground content.
* Buttons should feature a subtle liquid fill or color-sweep animation on hover.
